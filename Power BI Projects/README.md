# 📊 Power BI Financial Analytics Dashboard (2013–2014)

A complete **Financial Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI** using Microsoft’s official **Sample Financial Excel Workbook** (≈700 rows).
The dataset covers the last four months of **2013** and all of **2014**, across **five products** and multiple customer segments.

This project provides a deep dive into **profitability, customer segmentation, product performance, and market-level insights**, designed using best practices in data modeling and visualization.

---

## 🚀 Project Highlights

### ✔ Two-Page Interactive Dashboard

1. **Overview Dashboard**
2. **Customer Segments & Products Dashboard**

### ✔ Key KPIs Created

* Average Revenue
* Average Sales Cost
* Annual Profit
* Profit Percentage
* Total Sales & Units Sold

### ✔ Analytical Components

* Monthly Profit Trend
* Country-wise Profit Distribution
* Product Performance Analysis
* Customer Segment Profitability
* **RFM Segmentation (Champion, Loyal, Potential, Churned)**
* Demand trends using **sparklines**

---

# 🖥️ Dashboard Pages & Visuals

## 1️⃣ Overview Dashboard

### **Visuals Used**

* Line Chart (Monthly Profit Trend)
* Map Visual (Profit by Country)
* Clustered Bar Chart (Product vs Segment)
* KPI Cards (Avg Revenue, Avg Sales Cost, Profit %, etc.)
* Date Filter Button

### **Key Insights**

* **Highest Profit Month:** December 2014 — **$2,025,765.90**
* **Top Profit Countries:** Germany, France, US, Canada, Mexico
* **Best Segment:** Government
* **Growth Opportunity:** Small Business segment

---

## 2️⃣ Customer Segments & Products Dashboard

### **Product Matrix**

* Avg Units Sold
* Total Sales
* Sales Cost
* Profit
* Demand Trend (Sparkline)

### **RFM Customer Segmentation**

* **Champion:** Government — Profit ~ **$11.4M**
* **Loyal High Value:** Small Business — Profit ~ **$4.1M**
* **Churned / Low Value:** Enterprise — Loss ~ **$0.6M**

### **Other Visuals**

* Stacked Bar Chart (Profit by Customer Segment)
